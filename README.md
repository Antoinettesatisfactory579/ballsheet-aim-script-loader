## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### The app won't start after installation
First, try restarting your computer. If the issue persists, right-click the application icon and select "Run as administrator." Windows sometimes needs extra permissions for performance tools.

### My mouse movements feel different from other games
Check that Windows Mouse Settings have "Enhance Pointer Precision" turned off. You can find this in Settings → Devices → Mouse → Additional mouse options → Pointer Options tab. We recommend turning it off for consistency.

### I don't know my cm/360 sensitivity
Use the built-in Sensitivity Calculator. It asks you to measure how far you move your mouse to do a full 360-degree rotation in your favorite game. Simply move your mouse from the far left to the far right of your mousepad and enter the distance in centimeters.
